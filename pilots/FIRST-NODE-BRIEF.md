# First-node brief: research specification

**Status: questions to answer; no candidate selected.**

## The decision

What is the smallest credible starting point that meaningfully improves residents' lives and can expand without depending on unproven technology or unsupported financial promises?

Compare at least a modest parcel cluster, a node near an existing town, and a larger planned settlement if the latter remains plausible. An existing town is not an asset whose residents or government can simply be acquired.

## Required sections of a completed brief

1. **People and purpose:** intended households, their alternatives, actual problems solved, and the proposed benefit after all costs.
2. **Place:** boundaries, access, land control, legal uses, water, hazards, regional services, neighbors, and room to expand.
3. **First occupied phase:** number and type of units, services available on day one, approvals, schedule, and conditions before occupancy.
4. **Productive engine:** customers, enterprise mix, outside revenue, conventional staffing, demonstrated technology, and operating costs.
5. **Sources and uses of capital:** acquisition, site works, homes, utilities, services, startup losses, working capital, contingency, and financing terms.
6. **Ownership and governance:** institutions, rights, public authority, resident entry/exit, existing residents, conflicts, recourse, and open legal questions.
7. **Household outcomes:** complete budgets by cohort, cash and in-kind benefits, taxes and fees, time, reliability, assets, and risks.
8. **Resource and service budget:** water, energy, food procurement/production, transport, healthcare, schools, care, staffing, and replacement.
9. **Downside and alternatives:** failure conditions, cost overruns, no anchor, slow growth, delayed automation, and options to change direction.
10. **Decision and next evidence:** what is supported, what remains unknown, the most valuable next investigation, its bounded scope, and decisions requiring the operator.

## Proposed progression

Concept alternatives -> regional screen -> candidate dossiers -> integrated pre-feasibility -> professional/site validation -> separately authorized commitments -> first occupied phase -> measured operating results -> expansion decision.

These are research milestones, not authorization gates imposed by a software harness. They describe the evidence needed to justify increasingly consequential choices. No financial return, tax rate, service level, or expansion schedule has been committed.
