# Foundation scope and acceptance

Date: 2026-10-25. Requested by the operator in [D-001](../coordination/DECISIONS.md).

## Intent

Make the full regional-homestead vision understandable and give future contributors a serious, shared starting point for research. Preserve the operator's corrections: brainstorm inputs are exploratory; economic mechanisms and constraints need analysis; concise READMEs should orient specialist work without overconstraining the harness.

## Implementation choices

Use a documentation-first repository with canonical vision files, 16 research department charters, a separate digital-twin workspace, shared research registers, and coordination records. Include economics and pilot specifications so the organization points to concrete work. Keep AGENTS.md short and tool-neutral. Add templates that can be adapted to a task.

## Deliverables / acceptance

- Full vision and its evolution, living arrangements, enterprise/ownership intent, regional connections, technology, services, and simulation goals are captured.
- Each department has a distinct role, first questions, first deliverable, evidence needs, interfaces, and a reason to challenge an attractive assumption.
- Shared context separates intent, observations, assumptions, hypotheses, proposals, findings, decisions, and authorization.
- A task can be started, finished, checked, and handed off through the shared agent log without rereading a private chat.
- First-node, accounting, and model specifications identify actual next research questions and failure conditions.
- Existing assets and reported achievements are labeled by verification state; no feasibility or partnership is invented.
- Relative document links, units, arithmetic examples, references, whitespace, and public-repository boundaries are checked before publication.

## Deliberately open

Site selection, capital and ownership choices, legal/tax arrangements, benefit targets, technology stack, software implementation, artifact import, license selection, research assignments, fundraising, outreach, and real-world execution. They require their own evidence and scoped decisions; they are not inferred from repository setup.

## Publication

The repo was observed public and empty. Prepare on a task branch from a locally initialized main. Publish only after operator approval under the global ask-first rule, check for concurrent remote changes, and integrate through the branch/review workflow the operator authorizes.
