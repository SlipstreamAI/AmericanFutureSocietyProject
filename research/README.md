# Research common ground

Research is organized by [department](../departments/README.md), but shares one evidence and assumptions layer.

- [Agenda](AGENDA.md): the consequential questions and dependencies.
- [Method](METHOD.md): claim types, source quality, reproducibility, and review.
- [Assumptions](ASSUMPTIONS.md): founding scenario ideas and what would validate or replace them.
- [Source catalog](SOURCE-CATALOG.md): initial authoritative discovery sources, not a completed literature review.
- [Findings](FINDINGS.md): accepted links to completed work; currently no site or business feasibility study has been completed in this repository.

Use the [research brief template](../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Keep a department's detailed work beside its charter and promote cross-cutting conclusions into shared registers. Never infer that an unassigned backlog item is an instruction to start work.
