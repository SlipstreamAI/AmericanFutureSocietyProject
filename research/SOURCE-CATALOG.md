# Initial source catalog

Catalog date: 2026-10-25. The public pages below were located and inspected during foundation setup. This establishes useful starting sources, not extraction of site data, completed due diligence, or endorsement of a design. Retrieve the relevant release and metadata before using an input. Each entry's caveat describes how this project should use it.

## Sources to identify after selecting candidate jurisdictions

County and city zoning ordinances and comprehensive plans; adopted building and fire codes; parcel and title records; water authorities and adjudications; utility tariffs and capacity studies; road access and emergency-service records; state environmental and water agencies; tribal and federal land authorities where applicable; local budgets; school districts; insurers; actual construction and operating quotes; audited business or cooperative accounts.

These are required source categories, not a claim that they have already been collected. Avoid ranking locations before the high-consequence local evidence exists.
