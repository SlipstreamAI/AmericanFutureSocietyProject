# Priority research agenda

These are proposed bounded investigations. The operator chooses what to start; the ordering reflects dependencies, not a scheduled program.

| ID | Question | Lead department | Decision or deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q-001 | What is the smallest pilot that delivers a meaningful household benefit? | Synthesis and strategy | Compare parcel cluster, town-adjacent node, and larger settlement |
| Q-002 | Where is there a credible water, land-use, access, and servicing path? | Land and location; water and environment | Evidence-backed regional screen, then parcel dossiers |
| Q-003 | Which household types are served, and what is their full baseline budget? | Economics and household finance | Cohort budget ranges and explicit cushion definition |
| Q-004 | What can bring paying customers and earned income into the region? | Enterprise and employment | Independently costed enterprise cases and demand evidence |
| Q-005 | What money can reach community entities and households after obligations? | Economics; capital and ownership | Reconciled entity cash-flow bridge and downside cases |
| Q-006 | Who funds startup and expansion, and how are risks and returns allocated? | Capital and ownership | Staged capital stack, working capital, and ownership alternatives |
| Q-007 | What does the first occupied phase physically cost and require? | Housing and construction | Serviced-unit cost ranges, permits, schedule, and capacity |
| Q-008 | How can essential services work before ambitious new infrastructure exists? | Health and care; energy; mobility | Service map, realistic partners to investigate, capacity and cost |
| Q-009 | What governance protects choice, dissent, exit, and existing residents? | Governance and legal | Institutional alternatives and jurisdiction-specific questions |
| Q-010 | Which automation cases remain viable with present-day technology? | Technology and automation | Capability evidence and staffed fallback economics |
| Q-011 | Which assumptions most change feasibility or break essential services? | Risk and resilience | Shared risk register and correlated stress scenarios |
| Q-012 | What must the model explain, conserve, and validate? | Digital twin; research and evidence | Model contract, benchmark plan, and uncertainty disclosure |
| Q-013 | Can crop hosting or local food production improve household outcomes? | Food and agriculture | Crop-specific unit economics compared with purchasing food |
| Q-014 | What are the actual cost and access implications of dispersed parcels and RV mobility? | Mobility; housing; land | Access, tenure, travel-time, and infrastructure comparison |
| Q-015 | How do existing residents, newcomers, families, and people needing care fare over time? | Education and community; governance; economics | Distributional outcomes and real engagement plan |
| Q-016 | Which incentives reward business contribution without being easily gamed? | Governance; enterprise; economics | Transparent scorecard alternatives and implementation levels |

## Initial synthesis package

The first serious recommendation should connect Q-001 through Q-009 in one [first-node brief](../pilots/FIRST-NODE-BRIEF.md). It should include at least one viable-looking alternative, the best competing alternative, evidence that could rule each out, and the cost of resolving the biggest unknowns. A model should expose these questions before elaborate visualization is treated as validation.
