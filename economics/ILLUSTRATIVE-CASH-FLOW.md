# What the founding numbers would mean

**Arithmetic demonstration only, dated 2026-10-25. These are not forecasts, documented enterprise economics, investment returns, or adopted resident benefits.** The inputs are [A-002 through A-004 and A-012 through A-015](../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md).

## From $1.8 billion in sales to resident cash

Assume 50,000 recipients, $1.8 billion annual business sales, a 10% margin of cash available for owner distribution after all enterprise obligations, and a community right to 25% of that cash:

| Step | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible enterprise cash | $1,800,000,000 x 10% | $180,000,000 per year |
| Community receipt | $180,000,000 x 25% | $45,000,000 per year |
| Per recipient before community spending | $45,000,000 / 50,000 / 12 | $75 per month |

That $75 is before the community entity's own services, administration, reinvestment, and reserves. The assumed margin is already after enterprise obligations; the example must not silently subtract the same costs twice. Actual margins and rights need business-specific evidence.

At the same assumptions, an illustrative $1,500 monthly payment to all 50,000 recipients would require $900 million per year of distributions. That implies $36 billion in annual business sales before additional community spending. This inversion reveals how much is being assumed; it is not a sales target or recommendation.

## What could $4.5 billion do?

The brainstorm did not establish whether this figure meant construction cost, financing, fund assets, or another quantity. Its meaning must be resolved before using it.

- **If it were construction capital**, it would buy specified assets and infrastructure. Household benefits would depend on the costs avoided and the assets' later net cash flows. Construction spending is not an annual dividend pool.
- **If it were an invested fund**, an assumed 4% annual withdrawal would be $180 million, or $300 per month for 50,000 recipients before other uses. That is a hypothetical rule, not a guaranteed sustainable investment return.
- **If it were debt financing**, its service and repayment would need funding. Financing received is not operating revenue.

For scale only, funding $900 million annually from an assumed 4% fund withdrawal would require $22.5 billion in assets before other costs. Inflation, market losses, fees, distribution policy, and reserves could materially change that relationship.

## The question this opens

A household's improvement may combine lower housing and utility costs, earned income, pensions, hosting income, and ownership distributions. Investigate each source and its costs instead of forcing every benefit to come from one universal dividend. Compare full household outcomes, realistic enterprises, and alternative node sizes using the [accounting contract](ACCOUNTING-CONTRACT.md).
