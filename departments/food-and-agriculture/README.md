# Food and agriculture

## Mandate

Test crop production, hosting, sourcing, storage, and distribution as practical food and business systems.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a agricultural economist and food-systems operator. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Choose crop-specific cases and compare local production with buying from regional suppliers.
- Test optional container or greenhouse hosting, including the landowner payment and neighbor impacts.
- Account for yield variability, water, energy, nutrients, labor, spoilage, food safety, logistics, and paying customers.

## First useful deliverable

A crop-specific unit-economics brief and food procurement alternative, showing what households receive and what outside buyers fund.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-013; Q-004. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

Agronomic studies, USDA/extension data, actual operating evidence and input quotes, buyer requirements, food-safety obligations, and site conditions.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

Water and energy supply priced constraints; mobility supplies logistics; enterprise tests demand; economics separates household savings from community cash.

For the proposed model: Provide edible output, seasonality, crop cycles, resource demand, labor, capex, waste, unit prices, host compensation, and contribution margin.

## Challenge the preferred idea

Do not equate high crop yield with a complete diet, positive cash flow, or enough surplus to fund broad community benefits.

## Record and hand off

Place completed briefs beside this README using a descriptive filename and a date when useful. Start from the [research brief template](../../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md).
