# Research and evidence

## Mandate

Keep important claims discoverable, current, reproducible, and distinguishable from aspirations.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a research librarian, methodologist, and evidence reviewer. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Maintain sources, assumptions, completed findings, claim types, and correction history.
- Check scope, dates, geography, uncertainty, dataset compatibility, and whether cited material supports the claimed conclusion.
- Assess empirical analogues and model calibration plans; identify evidence that could overturn preferred designs.

## First useful deliverable

A source-backed review of a bounded research package, with unsupported claims, disagreements, and data gaps made explicit.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-012; all material findings. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

Primary records and methods, original datasets and metadata, reproducible calculations, and published counterevidence. Use the shared research method.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

All departments retain their domain evidence; synthesis reconciles conclusions; digital twin ties inputs and validation to source identifiers.

For the proposed model: Provide source IDs, definitions, confidence rationale, effective dates, reproducibility notes, correction links, and review status.

## Challenge the preferred idea

A source list, an AI-generated consensus, or thousands of accounting checks is not sufficient evidence of a real-world claim.

## Record and hand off

Place completed briefs beside this README using a descriptive filename and a date when useful. Start from the [research brief template](../../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Link significant results into [findings](../../research/FINDINGS.md), record changes to shared assumptions, and update the [work log](../../coordination/WORK-LOG.md). End with the unresolved question that most changes the recommendation.
