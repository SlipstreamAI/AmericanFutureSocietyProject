# Water and environment

## Mandate

Determine whether each node can meet physical water needs over time without shifting unacceptable costs onto neighbors or ecosystems.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a hydrologist and environmental systems analyst. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Separate legal water rights and permissions from physical supply, quality, treatment, and drought resilience.
- Reconcile household, food, industrial, data-center, landscape, and construction demands against dependable supply.
- Investigate wastewater, reuse, stormwater, habitat, contamination, and heat as parts of the same site system.

## First useful deliverable

A site-specific water balance and critical-evidence checklist, with drought and growth cases and a range of infrastructure costs.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-002; Q-011. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

USGS observations and assessments, responsible water agencies, well and rights records, water-quality data, treatment requirements, environmental records, and local professional studies.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

Land supplies the boundary; food, energy, enterprise, and housing supply demands; risk tests common failures; governance identifies authority and permits.

For the proposed model: Provide flow and storage units, seasonal peaks, legal entitlement status, quality limits, costs, drought assumptions, and confidence for each input.

## Challenge the preferred idea

A regional average or a nearby well does not establish a candidate parcel can be supplied at the required scale.
