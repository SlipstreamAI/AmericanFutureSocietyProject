# Health and care

## Mandate

Investigate accessible, affordable care and elder independence using realistic capacity, staffing, and financing.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a health-services planner and care-access researcher. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Map existing primary, emergency, specialist, mental-health, rehabilitation, dental, and elder-care services.
- Compare access through regional services, transport, telehealth, local facilities, and later expansion.
- Assess preventive care and assistive technology using evidence, clinician accountability, optional participation, and privacy.

## First useful deliverable

A first-phase service and access plan with capacity, travel times, staffing, costs, payment mechanisms, and gaps.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-008; Q-015. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

Provider and service records, HRSA data, clinical guidance for any proposed intervention, payer rules, and qualified service/clinical review.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

Mobility supplies travel and accessibility; housing supplies accessible design; economics funds services; technology supports staff rather than assuming replacement.

For the proposed model: Provide covered services, provider capacity, hours, emergency arrangements, household costs, staffing, and explicit limits on what the node can offer.

## Challenge the preferred idea

A nearby facility or an AI demonstration does not establish timely care capacity, clinical safety, or an obligation to disclose medical data.

## Record and hand off

Place completed briefs beside this README using a descriptive filename and a date when useful. Start from the [research brief template](../../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Link significant results into [findings](../../research/FINDINGS.md), record changes to shared assumptions, and update the [work log](../../coordination/WORK-LOG.md).
