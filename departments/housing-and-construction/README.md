# Housing and construction

## Mandate

Translate living choices into legal, serviced, affordable places to live with maintainable infrastructure.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a development manager, housing researcher, and construction cost analyst. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Compare conventional, modular, manufactured, and printed structures on complete installed and serviced cost.
- Examine renter, homeowner, rural parcel, and RV cases, including accessibility and household growth.
- Plan what must exist before first occupancy and how roads, sanitation, power, maintenance, and common land are funded.

## First useful deliverable

A first-phase housing program and cost range by tenure and unit type, with approvals, schedule, site works, and replacement obligations.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-007; Q-014. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

Applicable local codes and permits, actual supplier and contractor scopes, comparable completed projects, local housing data, finance and insurance requirements.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

Land defines site conditions; water and energy define services; governance examines tenure; capital funds development; economics measures whole household costs.

For the proposed model: Provide occupied units and residents separately, density, usable and shared land, construction lead times, all-in costs, tenure charges, and maintenance.

## Challenge the preferred idea

A low shell price or a construction demonstration is insufficient if the installed home, occupancy path, and ongoing household charges are unknown.
