# Risk and resilience

## Mandate

Find how attractive whole-node plans fail and how people are protected when they do.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a systems risk analyst and resilience planner. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Identify correlated water, power, enterprise, finance, insurance, construction, governance, and technology risks.
- Test household and service outcomes under delays, lower margins, outages, drought, turnover, and missing anchor customers.
- Specify observable warning signs, fallback capacity, recovery costs, and conditions that would stop expansion.

## First useful deliverable

A concise linked risk register, stress-case specification, and failure-response comparison tied to the current candidate node.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-011; Q-012. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

Hazard records and methods, utility/service reliability, operating analogues, financial sensitivities, and credible engineering and insurance evidence.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

Every department supplies failure modes; economics measures cash and household effects; governance defines accountability; digital twin implements authorized cases.

For the proposed model: Provide scenario correlations, severity/range basis, affected services, reserve needs, recovery times, mitigations, residual risks, and evidence gaps.

## Challenge the preferred idea

A plan that only works with simultaneous favorable assumptions must not be described as robust or ready for implementation.

## Record and hand off

Place completed briefs beside this README using a descriptive filename and a date when useful. Start from the [research brief template](../../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Link significant results into [findings](../../research/FINDINGS.md), record changes to shared assumptions, and update the [work log](../../coordination/WORK-LOG.md). End with the unresolved question that most changes the recommendation.
