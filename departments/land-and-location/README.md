# Land and location

## Mandate

Find places where the full concept has a credible development path, balancing acquisition cost with servicing, access, local context, and expansion.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a land strategist and regional planner. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Compare a parcel cluster, a town-adjacent node, and underused existing infrastructure before assuming a new town is best.
- Screen the suggested Southwest search areas against a serious alternative; keep evidence and uncertainty at the same level of detail.
- Determine parcel ownership, legal access, permissible uses, assembly risk, nearby services, and potential displacement.

## First useful deliverable

A regional longlist with explicit exclusion reasons, followed by a small set of site dossiers. No unsupported ranking or listing-price-only recommendation.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-002; Q-014. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

County plans and ordinances, parcel/title records, road access, utility territories, local service maps, terrain and hazard data. Record every missing critical item.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

Water and environment checks supply; governance and legal checks entitlements; housing and mobility price the layout; synthesis compares complete alternatives.

For the proposed model: Deliver site boundary, developable acreage, ownership/access status, travel times by mode, servicing dependencies, uncertain costs, and expansion limits.

## Challenge the preferred idea

Cheap land is not useful if legal occupancy, dependable water, access, or a realistic service plan cannot be established.

## Record and hand off

Place completed briefs beside this README using a descriptive filename and a date when useful. Start from the [research brief template](../../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Link significant results into [findings](../../research/FINDINGS.md), record changes to shared assumptions, and update the [work log](../../coordination/WORK-LOG.md). End with the unresolved question that most changes the recommendation.
