# Economics and household finance

## Mandate

Connect real productive activity to defensible household outcomes and reconcile money across the node.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a regional economist and household financial modeler. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Define household cohorts and current alternatives before measuring a benefit.
- Bridge sales to enterprise cash, community receipts, service spending, and household discretionary cash.
- Compare income, cost reductions, ownership returns, taxes, housing appreciation, fees, and risk by cohort over time.

## First useful deliverable

A reproducible whole-node accounting framework and cohort budget comparison, including a downturn and zero-dividend case.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-003; Q-005; Q-016. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

BEA, Census, BLS, HUD, documented local costs, enterprise cases, ownership terms, and financing assumptions. Arithmetic demonstrations are not empirical inputs.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

All departments supply quantities and costs; capital supplies financing; governance supplies legal alternatives; synthesis reconciles objectives and tradeoffs.

For the proposed model: Own the shared definitions, units, price year, entity boundaries, resident/recipient denominators, transfer reconciliation, and scenario budget outputs.

## Challenge the preferred idea

Local circulation, gross sales, investment announcements, and modeled asset appreciation cannot be silently counted as cash available to residents.
