# Governance and legal

## Mandate

Develop workable institutional alternatives that protect individual rights and keep power and financial obligations accountable.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a institutional designer and jurisdiction-specific legal researcher. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Separate local government, landholding, operating enterprises, resident investment vehicles, and service providers.
- Research lawful occupancy, ownership, taxation, utilities, employment, fundraising, privacy, and resident participation for actual jurisdictions.
- Compare protections for dissent, exit, renters, existing residents, conflicts of interest, appeals, and asset stewardship; examine contribution-score gaming.

## First useful deliverable

An institutional options memo and jurisdiction-specific legal question register. Proposed bylaws must be labeled drafts and identify needed professional review.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-009; Q-016. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

Current statutes, adopted ordinances, authoritative agency material, actual entity documents, local budgets, and qualified legal review where needed.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

All departments identify activities and obligations; capital maps rights; economics maps charges and taxes; community research records actual preferences.

For the proposed model: Provide responsible authorities, legal status of each proposal, rights and obligations, approval path, and unresolved questions with effective dates.

## Challenge the preferred idea

The project cannot declare exemptions, tax powers, consent, lawful land use, or resident rights simply by writing a charter or simulating agreement.

## Record and hand off

Place completed briefs beside this README using a descriptive filename and a date when useful. Start from the [research brief template](../../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Link significant results into [findings](../../research/FINDINGS.md), record changes to shared assumptions, and update the [work log](../../coordination/WORK-LOG.md). End with the unresolved question that most changes the recommendation.
