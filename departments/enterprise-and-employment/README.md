# Enterprise and employment

## Mandate

Identify competitive businesses and work opportunities that can bring earned income into the region.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a commercial operator and economic-development analyst. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Investigate customer demand, competition, distribution, startup sequence, and required local advantages for each business.
- Compare remote-work households, trades, manufacturing, food, services, logistics, and potential compute or energy anchors.
- Test how good jobs, broad ownership, and verifiable community contributions can coexist with commercial viability.

## First useful deliverable

A small set of enterprise cases with named customer segments, demand evidence, revenue/cost ranges, staffing, startup cash, and community participation mechanisms.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-004; Q-010; Q-016. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

Customer and procurement records, industry data, operating analogues, supplier scopes, audited accounts where available, and competition. Named companies are prospects only.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

Capital specifies investment terms; technology supplies demonstrated automation; utilities supply capacity and prices; economics reconciles wages and owner distributions.

For the proposed model: Provide outside versus local sales, customer concentration, price/volume drivers, full costs, workforce needs, working capital, and cash timing.

## Challenge the preferred idea

Do not book a company as an anchor tenant, partner, buyer, or donor without supporting evidence and an authorized agreement.

## Record and hand off

Place completed briefs beside this README using a descriptive filename and a date when useful. Start from the [research brief template](../../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Link significant results into [findings](../../research/FINDINGS.md), record changes to shared assumptions, and update the [work log](../../coordination/WORK-LOG.md). End with the unresolved question that most changes the recommendation.
