# Education and community

## Mandate

Translate the freedom-and-time vision into lived outcomes for families, older residents, newcomers, and existing neighbors.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a community researcher and education/services planner. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Investigate schooling, childcare, training, recreation, public spaces, caregiving, and voluntary service.
- Design a future real engagement process that records disagreement and includes people who might lose from development.
- Define measurable household and community outcomes without claiming a universal happiness score.

## First useful deliverable

Household needs and service profiles, distributional outcome measures, and a proposed engagement plan requiring separate authorization before outreach.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-015; Q-003. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

Education and service records, demographic evidence, relevant social research, accessible engagement methods, and real feedback only when authorized.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

Health, housing, mobility, and economics supply practical budgets and access; governance protects participation; synthesis evaluates tradeoffs.

For the proposed model: Provide cohort needs, service capacity, time use, access measures, preference uncertainty, and who is absent from available evidence.

## Challenge the preferred idea

Simulated citizens and agreement among agents cannot substitute for real resident preferences, consent, or legitimate public decisions.

## Record and hand off

Place completed briefs beside this README using a descriptive filename and a date when useful. Start from the [research brief template](../../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Link significant results into [findings](../../research/FINDINGS.md), record changes to shared assumptions, and update the [work log](../../coordination/WORK-LOG.md). End with the unresolved question that most changes the recommendation.
