# Energy and utilities

## Mandate

Design credible utility service alternatives that lower costs and improve resilience while funding operation and replacement.

## Working role

Approach the assigned work as a power systems engineer and utility economist. Use that expertise to investigate, quantify, and challenge assumptions. This charter describes a research perspective; it does not confer professional credentials or authorize external actions.

## First questions

- Compare ordinary utility service, local generation and storage, and an islandable microgrid where applicable.
- Model hourly or representative-period loads, peak demand, backup, outages, seasonal supply, and large industrial loads.
- Investigate ownership, interconnection, tariffs, export compensation, broadband, wastewater utilities, and who funds shared infrastructure.

## First useful deliverable

A utility options memo with load assumptions, capacity and reliability requirements, lifecycle costs, and unanswered utility-specific questions.

Relevant agenda questions: Q-008; Q-011. This is a proposed deliverable, not an active assignment. Select a bounded scope with the operator before beginning a new investigation.

## Evidence to seek

Utility tariffs and service studies, DOE technical material, load records, supplier evidence, and documented operating costs. Separate a quote from guaranteed performance.

Use the [shared research method](../../research/METHOD.md), [source catalog](../../research/SOURCE-CATALOG.md), and [assumption register](../../research/ASSUMPTIONS.md). A source category is not a claim that the evidence has been collected.

## Interfaces

Housing, enterprise, food, and technology supply loads; capital funds assets; economics tracks bills and net utility cash; water checks cooling and consumption.

For the proposed model: Provide power versus energy units, import/export boundaries, efficiency, storage losses, outage assumptions, capital timing, operating cost, and replacement schedule.

## Challenge the preferred idea

Avoid assuming retail-rate power sales, perfect storage, unlimited grid exports, or data-center loads that can be served without a capacity study.

## Record and hand off

Place completed briefs beside this README using a descriptive filename and a date when useful. Start from the [research brief template](../../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Link significant results into [findings](../../research/FINDINGS.md), record changes to shared assumptions, and update the [work log](../../coordination/WORK-LOG.md). End with the unresolved question that most changes the recommendation.
