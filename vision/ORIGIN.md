# Origin and evolution

This is an edited project brief based on the operator's founding discussion of 2026-10-25. It preserves the direction of the discussion without publishing a private transcript. It establishes intent, not external evidence of feasibility.

| Stage | What emerged | What remains open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared prosperity | Reward verifiable business contributions and give people a claim on technological gains | Incentive design, ownership, measurement, funding, and accountability |
| A place to test it | Explore a town or region combining productive businesses and better essential services | Location, local consent, legal structure, capital, and operating economics |
| Regional homestead | Offer several ways of living, including rural acreage, town life, hosting, and seasonal RV sites | Density, actual costs, water, access, and lawful development paths |
| Simulation | Visualize decades of change and follow hypothetical households | Data, behavioral assumptions, calibration, uncertainty, and explanatory design |
| Presentation and prototype | A deck and website were produced before the underlying research was sufficiently developed | Artifact audit and reconciliation with a defensible model |
| Research foundation | Organize departments, shared context, and a work log around a real feasibility investigation | Evidence-backed alternatives and a first-node recommendation |

## The correction that governs this foundation

The operator explained that conversational ideas were being repeated as presentation copy before the hard questions had been answered. Examples such as 50,000 residents, $1.8 billion in business revenue, and $4.5 billion needed mechanisms, definitions, and analysis. They were not settled project requirements.

The resulting direction is to preserve the ambition, investigate the constraints, compare alternatives, and explain what is achievable and how. Department work should refine or challenge the brainstorm. It should not merely restate it.

## Source boundary

The operator approved setting up the vision, research departments, READMEs, and shared agent log. The organizational layout in this repository is an implementation choice for that request. Economic, legal, location, technology, and governance proposals remain research material unless the [decision register](../coordination/DECISIONS.md) records a specific operator decision.
