# Shared vocabulary

| Term | Working meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Regional homestead | A region supporting several living arrangements through shared access to productive activity, infrastructure, and services |
| Node | A geographically defined pilot or participating region; its boundary must be stated in each analysis |
| Household | A group sharing a budget; not interchangeable with residents, adults, workers, or dividend recipients |
| Outside revenue | Payments from customers outside the modeled node for goods or services; distinguish transfers, financing, and asset sales |
| Enterprise sales | Gross customer receipts on the chosen accounting basis; not profit, GDP, or community income |
| Community cash receipt | Actual cash received by a defined community entity through ownership, contracts, charges, or other stated mechanisms |
| Distributable cash | Cash available for a stated use after obligations, reinvestment, reserves, and restrictions; define the entity and period |
| Household cushion | Cash remaining after a declared household budget of essentials, taxes, required payments, and debt service |
| Ownership dividend | A payment attached to an explicit ownership entitlement; different from wages, a service benefit, or means-tested aid |
| Public contribution scorecard | A research proposal for evaluating business benefits and costs transparently; no adopted score or tax policy exists |
| Digital twin | A model connected to a defined real system and observations; the initial deliverable is a scenario model specification |
| Scenario | A stated set of assumptions and interventions used to explore outcomes, not a promise or a forecast |
| Sweet spot | A set of feasible tradeoffs among household benefit, cost, risk, freedom, and resource capacity; no optimum is established |
| Approved | Explicitly adopted by the operator for a defined purpose; approval of a document is not approval to execute its proposals |
