# American Future Society Project

**A research foundation for a modern regional homestead: affordable essentials, meaningful ownership, productive enterprise, and more freedom over how people live.**

The project asks whether a voluntarily joined community can use better planning, technology, and ownership arrangements to give ordinary people greater financial security and more time for family, learning, and community life. A first node could combine homes, rural parcels, businesses, food and delivery hubs, resilient utilities, and access to existing regional services. Successful nodes might eventually connect across the country.

This repository holds the vision, research questions, evidence, economics, simulation design, and shared project memory. The immediate job is to establish what a viable first node would require, how it could start small, and what would have to be true before expanding.

**Stage: research foundation.** No site, population optimum, financing structure, resident payment, company partnership, or operating community has been established here. Numbers from the founding brainstorm remain scenarios to investigate. A polished simulation or a balanced accounting identity alone cannot establish feasibility.

## The questions that matter first

- Where can people legally and sustainably live the proposed ways, with dependable water and access to essential services?
- What is the smallest useful pilot, and what does each stage cost before it earns revenue?
- Which customers outside the node pay for its businesses, and what cash can the community actually retain?
- How do households benefit after all bills, financing costs, taxes, and reserves?
- What ownership and governance arrangements protect both individual freedom and long-term community assets?
- Which technologies work today, which are optional experiments, and what happens if automation arrives late?
- What evidence would make us change direction or decide a proposed node does not work?

See the [illustrative cash-flow bridge](economics/ILLUSTRATIVE-CASH-FLOW.md) for why business sales and resident income are different quantities, and the [first-node brief](pilots/FIRST-NODE-BRIEF.md) for the research sequence.
